A QUICK HOUSE TOUR

Chase 21.

Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Choose a bet, then deal yourself in.

Hit / H
Take another card. At 21, your hand stands automatically.
Stand / S
Keep your hand and let the dealer play.
The cards
Face cards count as 10. Aces count as 1 or 11, whichever helps your hand.
The payout
A win pays 1:1. A two-card blackjack pays 3:2. A tie returns your whole bet.
House rules
One fresh deck per hand. Dealer stands on soft 17. No splitting, doubling, or insurance.

Start with 1,000 pretend chips. Bets lock when you deal. Refreshing starts a new session; if you run out, take a fresh start.
